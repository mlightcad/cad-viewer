# Continuous Distance

Continuously measure the distance between multiple points, with each segment displaying its length independently.

## Interaction Steps

1. Click the first point
2. Click subsequent points in order—each click immediately creates a new segment and shows its length
3. Press **Enter** or **Esc** to finish (or click near the start/end point to auto-close)

## Preview

A full polyline rubber band, with a length badge at the midpoint of each segment.

## Limit

At most 100 points. Each pair of adjacent points is effectively an independent distance measurement that can be edited individually.
